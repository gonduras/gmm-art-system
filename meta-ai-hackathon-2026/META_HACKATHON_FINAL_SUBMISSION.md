## I. The Core Innovation: The Multi-Model Assemblage

In multidisciplinary analog collage, an artwork is not a single flat texture: it is an **assemblage** of disparate physical materials—heavy 350 gsm cotton rag cardstock, butcher anatomical diagrams, found newsprint, medium-format Rolleiflex photographic prints, and bent copper wire—cut with scalpel and scissors across Tel Aviv and Johannesburg.

In our sovereign factory, we treated AI models not as unified black-box image generators, but as **specialized computational craftsmen reading the artist's work from different perspectives**:
- **Meta SAM-3.1 & SAM-3D:** Segmented the organic paper contours and proposed internal volumetric masses.
- **Meshy:** Generated dense sculptural planar facets for character bodies.
- **Meta `muse-spark-1.3`:** Acted as the spatial kinematic compiler, calculating exact matrix inverse transformations (`attachment.js`), OpenUSD stages (`frankenstein_stage.usda`), and rigid 24fps hinge tables (`motion_tables_p06_p15.json`).
- **Meta `muse-wonder-1.0`:** Acted as the uncompromising theatrical director and material critic, auditing figure-ground tension, Void Theatre lighting, and honestly rejecting drifting video diffusion.
- **Codex & AGY:** Enforced mathematical proofs, closed kinematic attachment loops, and assembled the disparate readings into a unified Three.js WebGL spatial stage.

The resulting artwork is a **meta-collage**: an assemblage of the distinct forms that different AI models read from human art, reconciled under the sole visual authority of the artist's hand.

## II. The Technical Breakthrough: Preserving Paper Materiality Against the 3.5-Second Video Collapse

Standard generative video diffusion models fail catastrophically when applied to physical collage. In our empirical testing:
1. **The 3.5-Second Video Collapse:** Autoregressive diffusion models hold paper texture for roughly 2.0 to 3.0 seconds. Past 3.5 seconds, scissor cuts soften, halftone newsprint dissolves into airbrushed gradients, and physical paper melts into rubbery CGI plastic.
2. **The Ceramic Smoothing Trap (The P13 Control):** Off-the-shelf 3D reconstruction algorithms interpret paper fibers and scissor seams as surface noise, smoothing them into glossy porcelain figurines.

### Our 3-Fold Defense:
1. **Pristine Paper Skin (+Y Front):** The front face of every creature is 100% the authoritative, high-resolution physical paper scan from Alexander's layered PSD. No algorithm is permitted to overwrite the printed paper surface.
2. **Deep Spatial Parallax (Three Physical Planes):**
   - **Deep Sky ($Z = -0.220\text{ m}$):** Clean atmospheric background, eliminating clipping and ghosting.
   - **Midground Cliffs ($Z = -0.075\text{ m}$):** Structural cardstock rock strata with cyan architectural wireframes.
   - **Foreground Birds ($Z = 0.000\text{ m}$):** The 6 strange asymmetrical birds with the newly attached fish tail gripped firmly in the orange bird's beak.
3. **Internal Copper Wireframe Armature ($Z = -0.006\text{ m}$):**
   - Instead of draping an opaque wire-mesh grid over the front of the paper (which makes it look like "pixelated 3D"), the wireframe is docked **strictly behind/inside** the paper skin.
   - From the front (0°), you see pure analog collage.
   - When the camera orbits to 35° oblique, you discover the delicate, glowing copper puppet skeleton supporting the paper from behind.
